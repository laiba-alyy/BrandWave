AuraStyle Brand Knowledge Base & Customer Support Policies1. Shipping and Delivery PoliciesStandard Shipping Timeline: AuraStyle processes and ships all standard orders within 2 to 3 business days. Delivery takes 5 to 7 business days after shipment.Expedited Shipping: Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs a flat rate of $15.Free Shipping Threshold: Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders under $75 incur a flat $4.99 shipping fee.International Shipping: We currently ship only to the United States and Canada. International customs fees are the responsibility of the customer.2. Returns, Refunds, and ExchangesReturn Window: Customers can return items within 30 days of the delivery date.Item Condition for Returns: Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have the original tags attached. Sale items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.Return Shipping Fees: AuraStyle provides a prepaid return label, but a $5 return processing fee is deducted from the final refund amount.Refund Processing Time: Once the return package is received at our warehouse, refunds take 5 to 10 business days to credit back to the original payment method.Exchanges: Exchanges are completely free. Customers can initiate an exchange online for a different size or color of the same item without paying the processing fee.3. Order Management and TroubleshootingModifying or Canceling an Order: Orders can only be changed or canceled within 60 minutes of placement. After 60 minutes, the order moves to fulfillment and cannot be altered.Missing or Damaged Items: If an item arrives damaged or is missing from the package, customers must contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photo evidence to receive a free replacement.Tracking an Order: Customers receive a tracking number via email as soon as the order ships. They can also track packages by logging into their AuraStyle account and visiting the "My Orders" tab.4. Product Care and WarrantyFabric Care Instructions: To prevent shrinking, all AuraStyle cotton apparel should be washed in cold water on a gentle cycle and tumbled dry on low.Product Warranty: AuraStyle offers a 90-day limited warranty covering manufacturing defects on zippers, seams, and hardware. It does not cover normal wear and tear or accidental damage.5. Frequently Asked Customer Queries (FAQs)Q: Why is my promo code not working?A: Promo codes cannot be combined with other offers or applied to "Final Sale" items. Check the expiration date of the code.Q: Do you restock sold-out items?A: Core collection items are restocked every month. Seasonal collections are limited edition and will not be restocked once sold out.Q: What payment methods do you accept?A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Klarna installment payments.
